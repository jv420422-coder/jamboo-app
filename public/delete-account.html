Delete Jamboo Account

Request permanent deletion of your Jamboo account and associated account data.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone.

What will be deleted?

When your account deletion request is processed, Jamboo will delete or remove account-associated data that is eligible for deletion, including:

  • Your Jamboo user profile
  • Saved addresses
  • Shopping cart data
  • Account-associated ratings
  • Account-associated notifications
  • Firebase authentication account

How to request account deletion

To request deletion of your Jamboo account, click the button below and send the email from the email address associated with your Jamboo account.
