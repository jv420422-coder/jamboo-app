Privacy Policy

At Jamboo, we respect the privacy of our restaurant partners and are committed to protecting the personal and business information provided through the Jamboo Restaurant Partner application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it and how it may be retained when necessary to operate our services and meet legal, financial and regulatory requirements.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the services you use, we may collect the following information:

  • Restaurant owner or authorised representative name
  • Mobile number and email address
  • Restaurant name and business details
  • Restaurant address and location information
  • Business hours and operating information
  • Food menu, item prices and related business information
  • GST, FSSAI and other business registration information where provided
  • PAN, Aadhaar and related verification information where required
  • Bank account, IFSC and UPI details for payouts or settlements
  • Restaurant logo, photographs and verification documents
  • Order, sales, payout and transaction information
  • Support requests, feedback and communications with Jamboo
  • Device, application and technical information necessary to operate the app

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to create and manage your restaurant partner account and to provide Jamboo's restaurant partner services.

  • Verify and manage restaurant partner accounts
  • Display restaurant and menu information to customers
  • Receive and process customer orders
  • Coordinate order preparation and delivery
  • Process payouts, withdrawals and settlements
  • Provide customer and restaurant partner support
  • Maintain application security and prevent misuse
  • Improve the reliability and performance of our services
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements

3. Sharing of Information

Jamboo does not sell or rent your personal information.

We may share limited information with customers, delivery partners, payment or financial service providers, technology service providers and other authorised service providers when necessary to provide Jamboo services, process transactions, complete orders, provide support or comply with legal requirements.

Information displayed to customers may include restaurant information such as restaurant name, address, menu items, prices, photographs, business hours and ratings or reviews where applicable.

4. Payment, Payout and Financial Information

Bank account, IFSC, UPI and related financial information may be collected when required to process restaurant payouts, withdrawals or settlements.

Financial and transaction records may be retained for the period required for accounting, dispute resolution, fraud prevention, tax, legal or regulatory purposes.

5. Verification and Business Documents

Where verification is required, Jamboo may collect business registration information and identity or verification documents provided by the restaurant partner.

Such information is used for account verification, fraud prevention, compliance and other legitimate business purposes. Verification information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate legal, financial or compliance purposes.

6. Location Information

Your restaurant location may be used to display your business to nearby customers, improve delivery accuracy and provide better order management services.

7. Order and Business Data

Order history, sales information, restaurant performance, menu information and customer ratings or reviews may be stored to help you manage your restaurant and to operate Jamboo's services.

8. Third-Party Services

Jamboo may use trusted third-party services such as Firebase, payment providers and other service providers to support authentication, cloud storage, notifications, application functionality and payment processing.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy and security policies.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or destruction.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security of information.

10. Account Deletion

Restaurant partners can request permanent deletion of their account through the account deletion option available in the Jamboo Restaurant Partner application.

When an account is deleted, personal information that is no longer required may be permanently removed from active systems.

Some order, transaction, payout, financial, support, fraud prevention, dispute or legal records may be retained where required by applicable law, accounting requirements, regulatory obligations or legitimate business purposes.

11. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain order and transaction records, process settlements, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, provide support and comply with applicable legal, financial or regulatory requirements.

12. Your Rights

You may request updates to your restaurant profile and business information where applicable. You are responsible for ensuring that the information provided to Jamboo remains accurate and up to date.

13. Children's Privacy

The Jamboo Restaurant Partner application is intended for restaurant owners, authorised representatives and other business users. It is not intended for children.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, technology, legal requirements or business practices.

The updated version will be made available on this page.

15. Contact Us

If you have questions, concerns or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your information, please contact Jamboo through the support channels provided in the Jamboo Restaurant Partner application.

16. Effective Date

This Privacy Policy is effective from September 7, 2026.
