Delete Your Account

This page provides information about how Restaurant Partners can permanently delete their Jamboo Restaurant Partner account and associated personal information.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Please make sure you no longer need access to your restaurant partner account before proceeding.

How to Delete Your Account

  1. Open the Jamboo Restaurant Partner App.
  2. Log in to your restaurant partner account.
  3. Open your Profile or Settings section.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Read the deletion warning and confirm the account deletion.

What Happens When You Delete Your Account?

When account deletion is successfully completed, Jamboo will delete the account and personal profile information associated with the restaurant partner account where applicable.

Information that is no longer required for business operations will be removed or deleted according to our data handling practices.

Information That May Be Retained

Some information may need to be retained for a limited period where required for legal, regulatory, accounting, fraud prevention, dispute resolution or other legitimate business purposes.

Such information will be retained only for as long as necessary for the applicable purpose and will be handled according to Jamboo's privacy practices.

Data Associated With Your Restaurant

Restaurant profile information, business information, authentication information and other personal information associated with your account may be deleted as part of the account deletion process, subject to applicable retention requirements.

Historical order, transaction, settlement, support or other records may be retained where necessary for legal, accounting, operational or dispute-resolution purposes.

Need Help?

If you are unable to access your account or need assistance with account deletion, please contact Jamboo Support through the Help Center available in the Jamboo Restaurant Partner App.

Privacy Policy

For more information about how Jamboo collects, uses and protects information, please refer to the Jamboo Restaurant Partner Privacy Policy.

This account deletion information is effective from September 7, 2026.
