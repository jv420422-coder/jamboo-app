Jamboo Delivery Partner - Delete Account

This page allows Jamboo Delivery Partners to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

To request deletion of your Jamboo Delivery Partner account, please send an email from the email address associated with your Jamboo account to the official Jamboo Support Team.

Send your deletion request to:
hello.officialjamboo@gmail.com

Please include your registered name, registered email address or mobile number, and mention that you want your Jamboo Delivery Partner account and associated personal data deleted.

What Will Be Deleted

After verifying the request, Jamboo will delete or anonymize personal information associated with the account where deletion is applicable.

This may include:

  • Profile information
  • Contact information
  • Profile photograph
  • Identity verification information, where legally permitted
  • Vehicle information, where legally permitted
  • Location information associated with the account, where legally permitted
  • Other personal information associated with the Delivery Partner account

Information That May Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained where required by applicable law or reasonably necessary for legitimate purposes, including financial records, transaction records, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution and legal compliance.

Where retention is required, the retained information will be handled according to applicable privacy and data-protection requirements.

Deletion Requests

Jamboo may need to verify the identity of the requester before processing an account deletion request. This is done to help prevent unauthorized deletion of another person's account.

Once the request has been verified and processed, the account may no longer be available for use and the associated personal information will be deleted or anonymized as applicable.

Need Help?

If you have any questions regarding account deletion or your personal information, contact the Jamboo Support Team.
