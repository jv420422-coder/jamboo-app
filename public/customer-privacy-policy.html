Privacy Policy

At Jamboo, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information necessary to provide our food delivery services, including your name, mobile number, email address, delivery address, location when you choose to use it, order details, and payment-related information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to process orders, arrange deliveries, manage your account, communicate with you about orders, provide customer support, and improve our services.

3. Location Information

If you grant location permission, Jamboo may use your current location while you use the app to help you select a delivery address and find nearby restaurants. You may also choose a saved address instead.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share relevant information with restaurants, delivery partners, and payment service providers when necessary to process your orders, complete deliveries, and provide our services.

5. Payments

Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers. Payment information is handled according to the applicable provider's policies and procedures.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable security measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

7. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain information as needed to provide our services, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. You may contact Jamboo support to request account deletion and information about applicable data deletion procedures.

8. Third-Party Services

Jamboo may use third-party services for authentication, maps, notifications, payment processing, and app infrastructure. These services may process information as necessary to provide their functionality.

9. Children's Privacy

Jamboo is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with the revised content.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, please contact Jamboo Support through the Help Center in the app.
