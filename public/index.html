Privacy Policy

At Jamboo, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide our food delivery services, including:

  • Name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Delivery address
  • Order information
  • Payment-related information

2. How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is used to process orders, improve our services, communicate with you and provide customer support.

3. Sharing of Information

Jamboo does not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share limited information with restaurants, delivery partners and payment providers only when necessary to complete your orders and provide our services.

4. Data Security

We use appropriate security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse or disclosure.

5. Account Security

Customers are responsible for keeping their account credentials secure and confidential.

6. Anonymous and Aggregated Data

Jamboo may use anonymous or aggregated data to improve the application and user experience.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Continued use of the application after updates indicates your acceptance of the revised Privacy Policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact Jamboo Support through the Help Center.
